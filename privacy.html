Privacy Policy

Cyborg Studio MM · Effective 20 November 2026 · cyborgstudiomm.com

Cyborg Studio MM (“we”, “us”) builds Burmese voice tools, movie-recap tools and point-of-sale software. This policy explains what data our Facebook Messenger bot and our website collect, why, and how long we keep it.

1. Messenger bot

When you send a message to our Facebook Page (Cyborg Studio MM), our bot receives the message text and your public Messenger profile information (name and profile picture) through Meta’s Messenger Platform, and replies automatically.

  • What we use it for: only to generate the reply — greeting you, explaining our voice service and how to get a user key, and directing price questions to our admin. The bot never states prices.
  • What we do not do: we do not sell your messages or profile data, share them with any third party other than Meta’s platform (which delivers the messages), or use them for advertising.
  • Retention: message content is processed in memory to produce the reply. Short-lived diagnostic logs (message text and sender ID) are kept for at most 24 hours for debugging and then deleted automatically.

2. Website accounts and voice generation

Our website offers free tools and a voice-generation studio. Some features need a member account (Google, email or Telegram login). We store only what is needed to run the account: your login identifier, your user key, and your remaining character balance.

  • Text you submit for voice generation is sent to our voice server to produce audio, then discarded. We do not keep your text or your generated audio after delivery.
  • Voice cloning uses a short reference recording only when you provide one, and only for your own voice or a voice you have permission to clone.
  • We do not sell account data or generation history to anyone.

3. Cookies and analytics

Our website uses browser local storage to remember your preferences (theme, language, saved projects) on your own device. We do not run third-party advertising trackers.

4. Data deletion

To ask for a copy or deletion of your account data, or to ask any privacy question, contact us at cyborgstudiomm@gmail.com. We will respond within 7 days.

5. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above.
